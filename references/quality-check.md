# Quality Check

Inspect the finished image at full size and as a thumbnail.

## Source and join

Verify:

- the source photograph is unchanged;
- people, faces, objects, architecture, signs, lighting, and color remain
  unchanged in the source panel;
- panels match dimensions;
- the seam is straight, clean, and edge-to-edge;
- no frame, margin, shadow, tape, or separator has been added.

## Companion panel

Verify:

- the companion was generated as one complete panel;
- motif, title, swatches, and journaling lines do not look separately overlaid;
- the motif is derived from the specific source;
- one or two structural source relationships remain perceptible;
- the source has not become a detail-for-detail watercolor copy;
- the subject has not disappeared into generic atmosphere;
- the primary motif occupies about 42–48% of the panel;
- quiet space remains intentional and source-derived;
- the paper ground shares the source’s overall color temperature.

## Archive elements

Verify:

- exactly three readable irregular watercolor swatches;
- swatches are grouped and arranged as a calm value sequence;
- one concise handwritten English title is legible and secondary;
- title width is below about 35% of the panel width;
- journaling area contains 2–4 widely spaced empty low-contrast lines;
- title, swatches, and lines sit in quiet space without crossing the motif;
- no subtitle, caption, date, location label, seal, logo, watermark, or extra
  text appears.

## Final test

Ask:

> Does this feel like a preserved visual-memory artifact rather than a decorated
> photograph?

If not, simplify the companion panel before changing the untouched source photo.
